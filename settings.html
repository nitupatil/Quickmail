How to Connect
  1. Open your Google Apps Script editor.
  2. Paste the CRM backend script into Code.gs.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment.
  4. Select type: Web app.
  5. Execute as: Me.
  6. Who has access: Anyone.
  7. Copy the resulting Web App URL and paste it above.
